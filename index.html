Welcome to War Room

War Room is a single-page board for co-op sessions: goals, quick notes, coordinates, and a light trade list. It complements voice chat — it does not replace your launcher or Discord.

  • Mission — session details, objectives, checkpoints, and scratch notes.
  • Signal — short messages that stay on the board (intel, ETA, jokes).
  • Roles — who is doing what this session.
  • Waypoints — shared locations (e.g. coordinates).
  • Supply — simple need / offer list.
  • Vote — optional group pick (e.g. modpack).
  • Admin — export/import and optional cloud sync between PCs.

Pick yourself under Active operator, add a few objectives, then skim the tabs. Press ? for keyboard shortcuts.

Reopen anytime from the ☰ menu or the Tour button in the header.
